DISCOVERY DESIGN
- A normal-looking planned card named Quiet Discoveries has a very subtle text hint.
- Monday–Sunday: it is tucked among the last seven cards in In Development.
- Its position changes once per weekday (seven distinct positions) but the card design and location section remain identical.
- After 5–30 RANDOM MINUTES of the Hub being the focused, visible browser tab, a small muted-gold egg appears next to the card number.
- Students have 10 minutes of focused, visible Hub time to spot and click it.
- After that, the egg disappears and stays gone until the next Pacific calendar date, even if the page reloads.
- Once a student clicks the egg, their browser unlocks the permanent Pocket Critters launcher above the search.
- Browser profiles matter. Progress and unlocks are stored locally; Pet Passports can back up the game itself.

These rules use device-local storage, without collecting student information.
Do not launch until you are satisfied with the preview and your own classroom testing.
