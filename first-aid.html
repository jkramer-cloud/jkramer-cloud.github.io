Real emergency call

You are about to contact real emergency services, not practice. If someone may need immediate help, call now. You do not have to be certain.

No prank calls. Intentionally false reports can delay lifesaving help and may result in school discipline, criminal charges or response costs.
Continue to 911

If you call accidentally, stay on the line and explain the mistake. Do not hang up.

Can't reach 911?

Keep trying to get help. Do not delay lifesaving first aid.

  1. Try 911 even if your carrier has no signal. Another compatible network may connect. Tell responders your exact location.
  2. Ask a nearby adult or bystander to call from another phone and bring an AED if needed. At school, alert the office or nurse.
  3. If you cannot call, try texting 911 where supported. Give your location and emergency. If delivery fails, find another way to get help. Texting requires a connection.
  4. Outdoors, use an available satellite emergency SOS device, rescue beacon or rescue plan. If safe, send someone for help. Avoid additional danger.
  5. Continue appropriate first aid while help is sought. Follow the dispatcher or AED when available.

This app cannot send a distress signal, locate you, place a call on your behalf or guarantee a connection. For medical guidance, use established resources and training.
